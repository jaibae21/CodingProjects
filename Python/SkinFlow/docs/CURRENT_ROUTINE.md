# Current routine — October 25, 2026

This is the user's supplied planned routine, not a medical recommendation. It is installed once
into SQLite by `current_routine.py`; the UI reads database records. Existing products, personal
edits, previous plan templates, revisions, and completions are retained. Missing or renamed inventory
matches become reminders requiring manual selection.

The user's subsequent correction limits routines to **Morning and Night**. Evening and nighttime
share the Night session, and the previously generated Sunday midday treatment belongs in Night.

## Monthly AHA/BHA review

The first Saturday Night has an optional review-required rule for Cetaphil Gentle cleanser →
Cos De BAHA AHA 30% / BHA 2% peeling serum → Klairs calming cream. Until explicitly approved,
the normal mask session remains and the treatment appears as a review reminder.

Approval proposes replacing Saturday's mask and recovery overrides for Thursday/Friday before
and Sunday/Monday after. These remove recorded irritants and retain/fill cleansing and moisturizer
steps, keeping sunscreen last. All changes require review and approval. Skipping keeps the normal
session; skipped actives are never moved to another day. Follow product application/removal
directions and review actual skin comfort before use.
